INTRODUCTION
1. I am directed by the Secretary of State to draw your attention to the revision of the following
documents to correct minor errors:
a.
Approved Document B (fire safety) volume 1: Dwellings, 2019 edition
incorporating 2020 and 2022 amendments collated with 2025, 2026 and 2029
amendments
b. Approved Document B (fire safety) volume 2: Buildings other than dwellings,
2019 edition incorporating 2020 and 2022 amendments collated with 2025,
2026 and 2029 amendments
c.
2025 amendments to Approved Document B, volume 1 and volume 2
2. The revisions will come into force on 2 March 2025.  They do not affect the timelines set
out in Approved Document B (Fire Safety): new updates to support enhanced fire safety -
GOV.UK published on 02 September 2024.
3. The purpose of this Circular is to announce the revision of the following documents to
correct minor errors:
a.
Approved Document B (fire safety) volume 1: Dwellings, 2019 edition
incorporating 2020 and 2022 amendments collated with 2025, 2026 and 2029
amendments
b. Approved Document B (fire safety) volume 2: Buildings other than dwellings,
2019 edition incorporating 2020 and 2022 amendments collated with 2025,
2026 and 2029 amendments
c.
2025 amendments to Approved Document B, volume 1 and volume 2
APPLICATION OF THE APPROVED DOCUMENTS 
4. The changes made to the Approved Documents only apply to buildings and building work
in England.
TRANSITIONAL ARRANGEMENTS 
5. The revisions to the guidance come into force on 2 March 2025.  They do not affect the
timelines set out in Approved Document B (Fire Safety): new updates to support
enhanced fire safety - GOV.UK published on 02 September 2024.
APPROVED DOCUMENTS 
6. Approved Document B (Fire Safety) (2019 edition incorporating the 2020 and 2022
amendments collated with 2025, 2026 and 2029 amendments) has been updated with a
corrected version.
7. A detailed description of the amendments can be found in the circular letter published
here:  Building regulations: divisional circular letters - GOV.UK
8. Annex A to this Circular gives the Notice of Approval for the amended Approved
Documents.
9. Copies of the amended Approved Documents are available from:
https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/approved-documents, or
RIBA 
https://www.ribabookshops.com/
